Why we exist

Everyone’s connected.
Nobody’s together.

A generation is growing up through screens. Meeting someone new, holding an uninterrupted conversation, laughing around the same table: these are becoming rare skills. Social Play is a real-world playground built to bring them back.

01

A phone-free space

Phones stay in smart lockers at the door. Inside, your only device is a simple wristband watch.

02

A club that plays like a game

No grind for money. Show up, talk, be kind, and your points, level and perks grow.

03

A transparent social enterprise

Rent, wages, stock, profit and loss: every number is public. Surplus funds new clubs.

The meeting ritual

Tap. Bring close. Discover.

01 · MEET

You’ve been chatting with someone. You both double-tap your watches and bring them close.

02 · DISCOVER

Beyond a name: interests, club history and the groups you have in common.

03 · REFLECT

At home you rate each other and leave a short note. Next time you meet someone, their watch glows accordingly.

Mint · friendlyWarm and easy to talk to
Coral · energeticBrings the room alive, meets everyone
Violet · calm listenerLoves a deep conversation
White pulse · new memberNo history yet. Say hello!

Brightness shows how consistent that feedback is. Watches only ever show positive traits: negative feedback goes privately to the member and to moderation.

Emergencies

Phone-free, never unreachable.

01

Repeated calls

If the same number calls several times in a short time, we treat it as urgent.

02

Your watch buzzes

One vibration, one message: “Go to the phone room.”

03

Phone booths

Quiet, private booths where you can take the call properly.

04

It’s logged

Calls, messages and booth visits appear on your night report.

Peace of mind for families: you can always be reached. Clarity for members: you see how independent you really were tonight.

Play together

Everything you enjoy alone, done together.

We take the digital habits people love doing solo and turn them into group experiences for four to six people. All the tech, always shared.

FLAGSHIP

The Reels Room

Four to six people, one shared screen. Everyone has a Skip and a Stay button and the group average decides.

Now and then an AI asks the group a question. Your answers and votes shape what comes next, tuned to the character of your group.

When the scrolling stops, a conversation is left behind.

AI Quiz Night

Tables become teams; questions are generated around your interests.

Shared DJ

The room builds the playlist together, one vote at a time.

Team games

Escape rooms, giant table games and missions.

The Studio

Record podcasts and content as a group and share them with the community.

Points & levels

Being a good person pays off.

In a game, money isn’t what moves you forward: progress is. Show up regularly, hold real conversations, make new connections and create for the community. Students and young professionals earn on separate tracks, with extra rewards for professional growth.

LEVEL 1

New

Free entry, a welcome drink, the white-pulse glow.

LEVEL 2

Regular

Free drinks on set days.

LEVEL 3

Social

Bigger discounts, invitations to special events.

LEVEL 4

Premium

Top discounts, the right to design games and events.

LEVEL 5

Global

Every club worldwide, plus a voice in how the club is run.

Points can’t be bought. Exact criteria and thresholds will be published with our first rule book.

Radical transparency

Every penny in plain sight.

Income, costs, profit and loss are shown live on screens around the club and in the app. When we hit our target, donations stop and the surplus flows into a savings fund that opens new clubs.

Membership

Free. No referral. You are your own reference.

LOCAL

Your home club

Everyone joins free and starts at their local club. Points grow with consistency and behaviour; stay away too long and your level slowly drops.

GLOBAL

Every club

High-scoring members who’ve supported the fund can enter any Social Play club and help decide where the next one opens.

WHO CAN JOIN

18+, and 16+ with company

Everyone 18 and over. Ages 16–17 are welcome with an adult (18+) companion; access to licensed areas follows UK licensing rules.

Trust

A safe space and a fair system.

Data & privacy

UK GDPR compliant, clear consent, minimal data, delete any time. We never record locations or conversations.

Fair ratings

Only people who actually met can rate each other. Negative notes go to moderation, never on display.

Physical safety

Trained staff, clear emergency procedures and a one-tap “I need help” on every watch.

Open to all

Free entry, zero discrimination. Every crowd finds its people, and crowds meet each other too.
